# Notes interface assets

- `iphone-16-pro.png`: unmodified 1406 × 2822 Black Titanium iPhone 16 Pro bezel. Apple Design Resources, distributed by James Jingyi's device-frame collection: https://github.com/jamesjingyi/mockup-device-frames/blob/main/Exports/iOS/16%20Pro/16%20Pro%20-%20Black%20Titanium.png . Original resources: https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/ . The 1206 × 2622 screen starts at x=102, y=100; CSS positions live content in that opening without transforming the text.
- `../fonts/inter-latin.woff2`: Inter variable Latin subset, Google Fonts distribution of Rasmus Andersson's Inter. https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@100..900&display=swap . SIL Open Font License: `../fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt`. Apple platforms use their system font; Inter is the local fallback.
